LIVE POSITION

Use your real location and heading?

Navigator will ask your browser for location (to place the view on the real street) and, on iPhone, motion & orientation (to turn the view with your compass). Raw sensor samples stay in memory. Road-package coverage centers and map data are stored locally on this device.

The OpenStreetMap square around your position (800 m walking, up to 2 km when driving) is downloaded from Overpass or the OSM API, so that service learns your approximate area. No camera is used. No account, analytics or server of ours receives anything. Road packages covering a 25-mile target radius are downloaded from OSM via Overpass and stored on this device; the service sees the requested areas.

If permission is denied or no absolute compass exists, manual controls remain available for whatever is missing. Compass readings are magnetic and can be disturbed indoors or near vehicles.
SUN CROSS-CHECK

Align with a shadow.

Stand still beside a clear shadow cast by an upright object onto level ground. Hold your phone flat, screen up, with its top edge pointing from the object's base toward the shadow's tip. You do not need to look at the Sun.

If it is cloudy, indoors, or there is no clear shadow, close this check and keep using the compass.

Uses GPS, device time and your alignment. The difference can include magnetic declination, interference or alignment error. This cannot continuously detect drift. Correction clears after 10 minutes, 100 m of travel, stale sensors, screen rotation or leaving the app. No camera, storage or new network requests.
