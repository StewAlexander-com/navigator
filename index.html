LIVE POSITION

Use your real location and heading?

Navigator will ask your browser for location (to place the view on the real street) and, on iPhone, motion & orientation (to turn the view with your compass). Both are used only in memory on this device.

The OpenStreetMap square around your position (800 m) is downloaded from Overpass or the OSM API, so that service learns your approximate area. No camera is used. No account, analytics or server of ours receives anything, and nothing is stored after this session.

If permission is denied or no absolute compass exists, manual controls remain available for whatever is missing. Compass readings are magnetic and can be disturbed indoors or near vehicles.
